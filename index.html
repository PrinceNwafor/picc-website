Our Heart

A Church Where
Prevailers Are Raised

At PICC, we believe every child of God is born to prevail — in faith, in family, in purpose and in every sphere of life.

From our home on Ilaje Road in Bariga, we gather week after week to worship in spirit and truth, to hear God's Word boldly preached, and to carry His mercy and power into Lagos and the nations. Come as you are — you belong here.

Meet Our Lead Pastor

Pastor Elisha Esho

"My passion is to see souls saved and men and women come into the knowledge of the glory of God."

Pastor Elisha Esho is the visioner and lead pastor of The Prevailers International Christian Chapel, which he leads alongside his wife, Pastor Joyce Damilola-Dada. Together they shepherd a vibrant, Spirit-filled congregation devoted to revival, the undiluted Word of God, and raising kingdom prevailers for every sphere of life.
